GyroLock revision notes

Changes in this revision:
- Removed the opening animated zoom-out from the stabilized video renderer.
- Kept a constant 1.075x safety crop on the VIDEO so the short clip can still cover
  black/empty borders created by gyro stabilization.
- Added a subtle, directional motion-blur blend for the first 180 ms of VIDEO only;
  it fades quickly so the video becomes sharp.
- The still photo is written through the existing photo path and is not zoomed or
  processed by the video renderer.
- Gyroscope sensor source and the rest of the stabilization pipeline were not changed.

Important:
- This is a source ZIP, not a prebuilt APK.
- The project has not been compiled or device-tested in this environment. Build and
  test on the target Android/Gradle setup before installing.
